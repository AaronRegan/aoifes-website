How I can help

1:1 video consultations from the comfort of your home

  • Weaning support

    For preterm and term infants

  • Growth concerns

    e.g. faltering growth

  • Nutritional deficiencies

    e.g. iron deficiency, diagnosed or suspected

  • Gastrointestinal disorders

  • Women’s health

    Including nutrition for PMOS, fertility and pregnancy support

  • Vegan and vegetarian diets

About

Hi, I'm Aoife

pronounced “Eefa”

I’m a dietitian with six years’ experience in the NHS, supporting children and adults across a range of specialities, with a particular focus on paediatrics. Whether it’s for you or your little one, I offer practical, personalised and evidence-based advice to help you eat well and thrive.

Everyone’s situation is different, so it really matters to me that you feel listened to and heard.

Together, we’ll make sense of the nutrition advice out there and find an approach that works for you.

Credentials

  • HCPC registered
  • Member of the British Dietetic Association (BDA)
  • Recognised by the Swiss Red Cross (SRC)
  • BSc Nutrition and Dietetics, Glasgow Caledonian University
  • BDA-certified postgraduate module in Paediatric Nutrition and Dietetics
